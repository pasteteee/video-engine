# Chocolate Chip Cookie — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 5.7 cm tall, 5.8 cm wide, 3.1 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - tan `#e8b266` — about 10 m (252 st)
  - light brown `#cf8f45` — about 10 m (216 st)
  - pink `#f08a86` — about 5 m (36 st)
- Safety eyes 8 mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#151313`) — 2
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): brown `#5a3220`, dark brown `#3a1f14`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

## Pieces

### Cookie

Size before assembly: about 5.6 cm at the widest, 2 cm long; 14 rounds.

R1: with tan: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
R3: inc ×12 (24)
R4: (1 sc, inc) ×12 (36)
R5: (2 sc, inc) ×12 (48)
Change to light brown (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R5).
R6: 4 sc, (inc, 7 sc) ×5, inc, 3 sc (54)
R7–9: 54 sc (54) — 3 rounds
Change to tan (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R9).
R10: 4 sc, (dec, 7 sc) ×5, dec, 3 sc (48)
R11: (2 sc, dec) ×12 (36)
R12: (1 sc, dec) ×12 (24)
R13: dec ×12 (12)

→ Insert the safety eyes now (positions: see “Face”) and fix the backs. Start stuffing (firmly) and keep adding as you decrease.

R14: dec ×6 (6)

Top up the stuffing if needed. Fasten off leaving a 15 cm tail; with the needle weave it through the front loops of all 6 stitches of the last round, pull tight and hide the end inside.

### Cheek — make 2 (left and right, identical)

A flat circle about 1.5 cm across; 2 rounds.

R1: with pink: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Do not stuff.

## Assembly

Before sewing, mark the centre front of the round you need (the middle of the face / belly). “k st from the centre line” means count k stitches along that round. Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.
