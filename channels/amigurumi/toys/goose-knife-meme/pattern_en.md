# Goose with Attitude — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 12.4 cm tall, 10.3 cm wide, 10.5 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - white `#fbf8f2` — about 60 m (2093 st)
  - orange `#f7902e` — about 10 m (218 st)
  - grey `#b4b7be` — about 5 m (74 st)
  - dark grey `#4a4c52` — about 5 m (46 st)
- Safety eyes 8 mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#151313`) — 2
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): black `#151313`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

R3: (2 sc, inc) ×3 (12)
R4: 2 sc, (inc, 3 sc) ×2, inc, 1 sc (15)
R5: (4 sc, inc) ×3 (18)
R6: 1 sc, (inc, 2 sc) ×5, inc, 1 sc (24)
R7: (3 sc, inc) ×6 (30)
R8: 2 sc, (inc, 4 sc) ×5, inc, 2 sc (36)
R9: (5 sc, inc) ×6 (42)
R10: 3 sc, (inc, 6 sc) ×5, inc, 3 sc (48)
R11: (7 sc, inc) ×6 (54)
R12–21: 54 sc (54) — 10 rounds
R22: 4 sc, (dec, 7 sc) ×5, dec, 3 sc (48)
R23: 48 sc (48)
R24: 3 sc, (dec, 6 sc) ×5, dec, 3 sc (42)
R25: (5 sc, dec) ×6 (36)

→ Start stuffing (firmly) and keep adding as you decrease.

### Head and neck

Size before assembly: about 5.4 cm at the widest, 7.8 cm long; 24 rounds.

R1: with white: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
R3: (1 sc, inc) ×6 (18)
R4: 1 sc, (inc, 2 sc) ×5, inc, 1 sc (24)
R5: (3 sc, inc) ×6 (30)
R6: 2 sc, (inc, 4 sc) ×5, inc, 2 sc (36)
R7: 36 sc (36)
R8: 3 sc, (inc, 5 sc) ×5, inc, 2 sc (42)
R9–10: 42 sc (42) — 2 rounds
R11: (5 sc, dec) ×6 (36)
R12: 36 sc (36)
R13: (4 sc, dec) ×6 (30)
R14: 2 sc, (dec, 3 sc) ×5, dec, 1 sc (24)
R15–23: 24 sc (24) — 9 rounds
R24: 4 sc, (inc, 7 sc) ×2, inc, 3 sc (27)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Insert the safety nose/eyes now, while the piece is open (positions: see “Face”).
Stuff firmly just before sewing it on; a little less near the edge, then add a pinch when the seam is nearly done.
Bend ≈ 50° around round 15<!-- the bend spreads over 3 rounds -->. After stuffing, bend the piece so the inner side of the bend faces forward on the finished toy (on the piece: in round 15, on the centre front line). It is a gradual bend: with the needle and matching yarn run a line of stitches along the inner side from round 13 to round 17, one stitch in every round along one column, then pull the yarn until the piece bends to the angle (the inner side gathers by about 4 rounds) and fasten off inside.

### Toy dagger

Size before assembly: about 1.2 cm at the widest, 6.1 cm long; 17 rounds.

R1: with dark grey: 4 sc in MR (4)
R2: (1 sc, inc) ×2 (6)
R3–6: 6 sc (6) — 4 rounds
R7: inc ×6 (12)
Change to grey (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R7).
R8: (1 sc, dec) ×4 (8)
R9–15: 8 sc (8) — 7 rounds
R16: 1 sc, dec, 2 sc, dec, 1 sc (6)

→ Start stuffing (lightly) and keep adding as you decrease.

R17: (1 sc, dec) ×2 (4)

## Assembly

Before sewing, mark the centre front of the round you need (the middle of the face / belly). “k st from the centre line” means count k stitches along that round. Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.

1. **Body** is the base; its magic ring is at the back. <!-- Exact placement: axis (from the magic ring to the last round) 8.5° down, straight ahead. -->
2. **Head and neck → body**. Centre: in round 24, on the centre top line. Sew around the opening (27 st); the seam covers rounds 17–26 of the body, about 11 st wide. It points up. Press it into the base by about 5 mm. <!-- Exact placement: axis 73.3° up, straight back; round-start line faces 16.7° up, straight ahead; inner side of the bend faces 16.7° up, straight ahead; pressed in 5 mm. -->
3. **Beak → head and neck**. Centre: in round 11, on the centre front line. Sew around the opening (12 st); the seam covers rounds 8–12 of the head and neck, about 4 st wide. It points forward. <!-- Exact placement: axis 8.5° down, straight ahead; round-start line faces 81.5° up, straight ahead; pressed in 1 mm. -->
4. **Wing → body** (make 2). Centre: in round 16, 10.5 st from the centre top line, toward the toy's left (the one on the toy's left is described; sew the other as its mirror image). Flatten the opening (7 st against 7 st) and sew it on in a straight line top to bottom (along the body), along the round: rounds 15–17 of the body, about 8 st wide. It points backward and slightly outward. <!-- Exact placement: axis level, turned 155.8° to the toy's left; round-start line faces level, turned 65.8° to the toy's left; seam line 64.2° up, turned 114.2° to the toy's right. -->
5. **Tail → body**. Centre: in round 4, on the centre top line. Flatten the opening (5 st against 5 st) and sew it on in a straight line front to back, across the rounds: rounds 1–7 of the body, about 11 st wide. It points up and backward. <!-- Exact placement: axis 55° up, straight back; round-start line faces level, turned 90° to the toy's left; seam line 35° up, straight ahead. -->
6. **Foot → body** (make 2). Centre: in round 25, 1.5 st from the centre bottom line, toward the toy's left (the one on the toy's left is described; sew the other as its mirror image). Flatten the opening (6 st against 6 st) and sew it on in a straight line side to side (across the toy), along the round: rounds 24–26 of the body, about 4 st wide. It points forward. <!-- Exact placement: axis 8.2° down, turned 16.7° to the toy's left; round-start line faces 81.8° up, turned 16.7° to the toy's left. -->
7. **Toy dagger → body**. Centre: in round 22, 10.5 st from the centre top line, toward the toy's right <!-- st 10.4 right -->. Sew the piece on by its start (the magic ring side); it touches rounds 21–22 of the body, about 3 st wide. It points up. <!-- Exact placement: axis 72.2° up, turned 38.7° to the toy's right; round-start line faces 13.5° down, turned 2.8° to the toy's left; pressed in 2 mm. -->
