# Reindeer in a Sweater — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 16.5 cm tall, 12.9 cm wide, 8.5 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - brown `#a96b3f` — about 55 m (1816 st)
  - green `#2f7d4f` — about 20 m (602 st)
  - beige `#e6cfa0` — about 15 m (376 st)
  - cream `#f3e6d0` — about 10 m (292 st)
  - red `#d92b2b` — about 5 m (150 st)
  - dark brown `#6b3f26` — about 5 m (36 st)
  - pink `#f08aa0` — about 5 m (36 st)
  - white `#fbf8f2` — about 5 m (30 st)
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): black `#171314`, white `#fbf8f2`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- The face is fully embroidered — no hard parts, safe for small children.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |
| fpdc | front post dc: yarn over, hook around the post of the st from the front |
| bpdc | back post dc: yarn over, hook around the post of the st from the back |
| hdc | half double crochet |
| dc | double crochet |
| tr | treble crochet |
| picot | ch 3, sl st in the 3rd ch from hook |
| hdc2tog | 2 hdc worked together |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

### Body

R7: (5 sc, inc) ×6 (42)
R8: 3 sc, (inc, 6 sc) ×5, inc, 3 sc (48)

R9: (7 sc, inc) ×6 (54)
Change to green (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R9).
R10–14: 54 sc (54) — 5 rounds
R15: (7 sc, dec) ×6 (48)
Change to red (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R15).

### Star

Flat motif — star (worked in rounds, one layer of fabric), about 3.2 × 2.8 cm; 2 rows.

Round 1: with white: into a magic ring; 12 sc; sl st in the first st to join (12)
Round 2: (point (ch 3, then back along the chain from the 2nd ch from hook: 1 sc, 1 hdc), skip 1 st, 1 sl st) ×6 (18)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (~30 cm) for sewing. Do not stuff. Weave in the starting tail, pin the motif out to shape on a soft surface and steam it lightly.

### Ear — make 2 (left and right, identical)

Size before assembly: about 2.4 cm at the widest, 3.3 cm long; 10 rounds.

R1: with brown: 6 sc in MR (6)

Keep the opening a curve, hollow side forward, not a straight line.<!-- (spoon 55 %) -->

## Assembly

Before sewing, mark the centre front of the round you need (the middle of the face / belly). “k st from the centre line” means count k stitches along that round. Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.

1. **Body** is the base; its magic ring is at the bottom.
2. **Sweater hem → body**. Put the sweater hem around the body: the chain ring (54 st) lies along round 9 of the body, the rounds spreading down; sew the ring to the body all round and let the rest lie free on it. <!-- Exact placement: rounds grow straight down. -->
3. **Head onto the body.** Stuff the body firmly. Hold the head's closing point (last round) at the centre of the body's opening (R19) and sew the edge of the opening all round to the head: the seam runs along round 20 of the head (all the way round). Top up the neck with stuffing before closing the seam so the head does not wobble. <!-- Exact placement: axis straight up; round-start line faces level, straight ahead; pressed in 0 mm; pressed toward straight down. -->

### Face

The whole face is embroidered. Order: the eyes (black satin), then the white glints over them, then the nose and mouth, blush last. Mark the centres with pins first and compare with the picture. Bring thread ends out where a sewn-on piece will cover them, or bury them in the stuffing.

- **Satin stitch** (on the head) (2, symmetric: the toy's left side is described, the right is its mirror image): in black thread, a oval about 9 mm wide and 10.5 mm tall, horizontal stitches; centred in round 14, 4 st from the centre front line, toward the toy's left <!-- st 4.05 left -->. <!-- Exact: thread 1.5 mm thick. -->
