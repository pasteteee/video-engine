# Fox in Earmuffs Ornament — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 7.8 cm tall, 10.2 cm wide, 7.5 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - orange `#ee7a2e` — about 25 m (824 st)
  - white `#fbf9f4` — about 15 m (366 st)
  - red `#d63a34` — about 5 m (64 st + about 1.9 m for strands/chains/strips)
  - dark brown `#3b2a24` — about 5 m (36 st)
  - light pink `#ffa3b5` — about 5 m (32 st)
- Safety eyes 12 mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#1c1a1a`) — 2
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): black `#1c1a1a`, brown `#4a2f26`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |
| popcorn | 5 dc in one stitch; take the hook out of the loop, insert it in the first of the 5 dc, catch the dropped loop and pull it through - the dc gather into a bobble on the right side |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

## Pieces

### Head

Size before assembly: about 6.6 cm at the widest, 5.4 cm long; 22 rounds.
Multi-colour rounds are listed stitch by stitch from the round-start marker; keep the round start on the piece's centre front line (as it sits on the finished toy). The order is for right-handed crocheting (right to left on the outside); left-handed: reverse it.

R1: with orange: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
R3: (1 sc, inc) ×6 (18)
R4: 1 sc, (inc, 2 sc) ×5, inc, 1 sc (24)
R5: (3 sc, inc) ×6 (30)
R6: 2 sc, (inc, 4 sc) ×5, inc, 2 sc (36)
R7: (5 sc, inc) ×6 (42)
R8: 3 sc, (inc, 6 sc) ×5, inc, 3 sc (48)
R9–12: 48 sc (48) — 4 rounds
R13: 1 sc in white, 44 sc in orange, 3 sc in white (48)
R14: 2 sc in white, 41 sc in orange, 5 sc in white (48)
R15: [white] 4 sc [orange] 2 sc, dec, 6 sc, dec, 6 sc, dec, 6 sc, dec, 6 sc, dec [white] 6 sc, dec (42)
R16: 5 sc in white, 28 sc in orange, 9 sc in white (42)
R17: [white] 5 sc, dec [orange] 5 sc, dec, 5 sc, dec, 5 sc, dec, 3 sc [white] 2 sc, dec, 5 sc, dec (36)
R18: [white] 2 sc, dec, 2 sc [orange] 2 sc, dec, 4 sc, dec, 4 sc, dec, 4 sc [white] dec, 4 sc, dec, 2 sc (30)
R19: [white] 3 sc [orange] dec, 3 sc, dec, 3 sc, dec, 3 sc, dec, 1 sc [white] 2 sc, dec, 3 sc, dec (24)

→ Insert the safety eyes now (positions: see “Face”) and fix the backs. Start stuffing (firmly) and keep adding as you decrease.

R20: [white] 1 sc, dec [orange] 2 sc, dec, 2 sc, dec, 2 sc, dec, 1 sc [white] 1 sc, dec, 2 sc, dec, 1 sc (18)
R21: [white] 1 sc [orange] (dec, 1 sc) ×3 [white] dec, 1 sc, dec, 1 sc, dec (12)

R22: dec ×6 (6)

Top up the stuffing if needed. Fasten off leaving a 15 cm tail; with the needle weave it through the front loops of all 6 stitches of the last round, pull tight and hide the end inside.

### Ear — make 2 (left and right, identical)

Size before assembly: about 3 cm at the widest, 3.3 cm long; 10 rounds.
Multi-colour rounds are listed stitch by stitch from the round-start marker; keep the round start on the piece's centre front line (as it sits on the finished toy). The order is for right-handed crocheting (right to left on the outside); left-handed: reverse it.

R1: with dark brown: 4 sc in MR (4)
R2: (1 sc, inc) ×2 (6)
R3: (2 sc, inc) ×2 (8)
Change to orange (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R3).
R4: 2 sc, inc, 3 sc, inc, 1 sc (10)
R5: (4 sc, inc) ×2 (12)
R6: 3 sc, inc, 5 sc, inc, 2 sc (14)
R7: [orange] 6 sc, inc, 7 sc [white] 1 sc in same st (16)
R8: [orange] 4 sc, inc, 7 sc, inc, 1 sc [white] 2 sc (18)
R9–10: 16 sc in orange, 2 sc in white (18) — 2 rounds

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Stuff it lightly — a thin layer, just enough to hold the walls apart: the ear about 10.8 mm thick, with thick rounded edges. Then shape it into a spoon: push the front wall toward the back wall — a concave spoon about 3.1 mm deep in front, a rounded back; about 30 mm wide. Keep the opening a curve, hollow side forward, not a straight line.<!-- (spoon 50 %) -->

### Snout

Size before assembly: an oval about 2.2 × 1.8 cm at the widest, 1.1 cm long; 4 rounds.
The piece is oval: the rounds go around the chain, and on the finished toy the chain (the long side of the oval) runs side to side; the starting chain is at the front of the piece (on the finished toy). The round-start marker is in the middle of a long side (the piece's centre line), while R1 starts at a turn: after R1 shift the round start — sc 1 more (not counted in any round) and place the marker there. The increases fall on the turns.

With white: ch 3.
R1: starting in the 2nd ch from hook: sc 1, 3 sc in the last ch; continue along the other side of the chain: sc 0, 2 sc in the last ch (the one you made the first sc in) (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
R3: 2 sc, inc, 5 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc (15)
R4: 15 sc (15)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Stuff lightly just before sewing it on; a little less near the edge, then add a pinch when the seam is nearly done.

### Earmuff — make 2 (left and right, identical)

Size before assembly: about 2.4 cm at the widest, 2.2 cm long; 8 rounds.

R1: with white: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
R3: (1 sc, inc) ×6 (18)
R4: 18 sc (18)
R5 (popcorns): (1 sc, popcorn) ×9 (18)
R6: 18 sc (18)
R7: (1 sc, dec) ×6 (12)

→ Start stuffing (firmly) and keep adding as you decrease.

R8: dec ×6 (6)

Top up the stuffing if needed. Fasten off leaving a 15 cm tail; with the needle weave it through the front loops of all 6 stitches of the last round, pull tight and hide the end inside.

### Cheek — make 2 (left and right, identical)

A flat circle about 1.5 cm across; 2 rounds.

R1: with light pink: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: (inc ×2, 1 sc) ×2 (10)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Do not stuff.

### Hanging loop

Size before assembly: about 2 cm at the widest, 0.7 cm long; 2 rounds.
Each piece is two circles (front and back): make 2 circles — 1 as below and 1 plain in red (same rounds, no colour changes).

R1: with red: ch 16 and join into a ring with a sl st in the first ch (do not twist); 16 sc, one in each ch of the ring (16)
R2: 16 sc (16)

Fasten off. Hold a front and a plain circle wrong sides together and sc around through both layers: 16 sc. Do not stuff. Leave a long tail (~30 cm) for sewing.

## Assembly

Before sewing, mark the centre front of the round you need (the middle of the face / belly). “k st from the centre line” means count k stitches along that round. Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.

1. **Head** is the base; its magic ring is at the top. <!-- Exact placement: axis (from the magic ring to the last round) straight down. -->
2. **Ear → head** (make 2). Centre: between rounds 4 and 5, 3.5 st from the centre front line, toward the toy's left <!-- st 3.33 left --> (the one on the toy's left is described; sew the other as its mirror image). Sew around the opening (18 st); the seam covers rounds 2–8 of the head, about 7 st wide. Keep the spoon hollow side forward; the opening lies in a curve following the head, both layers sewn down. It points up and slightly outward. <!-- Exact placement: axis 63.3° up, turned 84.3° to the toy's left; round-start line faces 2.3° down, turned 1.1° to the toy's right; pressed in 1 mm. -->
3. **Snout → head**. Centre: between rounds 14 and 15, on the centre front line. Sew around the opening (15 st); the seam covers rounds 12–17 of the head, about 6 st wide. It points forward. <!-- Exact placement: axis 11.3° down, straight ahead; round-start line faces 78.7° up, straight ahead. -->
4. **Earmuff → head** (make 2). Centre: in round 11, 13 st from the centre front line, toward the toy's left <!-- st 12.8 left --> (the one on the toy's left is described; sew the other as its mirror image). Sew the piece on by its closed end (last rounds); it touches rounds 9–14 of the head, about 4 st wide. It points outward. 5. **Cheek → head** (make 2). 6. **Hanging loop → head**. Centre: at the centre of the magic ring.
