# Round Piggy — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 7.2 cm tall, 8.5 cm wide, 8.8 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - pink `#ffb3c1` — about 35 m (1190 st)
  - dark pink `#f08ba3` — about 10 m (202 st)
- Safety eyes 9 mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#151313`) — 2
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): brown `#6a3a3a`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Top up the stuffing if needed. Fasten off leaving a 15 cm tail; with the needle weave it through the front loops of all 6 stitches of the last round, pull tight and hide the end inside.

### Snout

A flat oval about 2.6 × 1.4 cm; 2 rounds.
Worked around a chain, so it is an oval: sew it on with its long side (along the chain) running side to side. The round-start marker is in the middle of a long side (the piece's centre line), while R1 starts at a turn: after R1 shift the round start — sc 2 more (not counted in any round) and place the marker there. The increases fall on the turns.

With dark pink: ch 5.
R1: starting in the 2nd ch from hook: sc 3, 3 sc in the last ch; continue along the other side of the chain: sc 2, 2 sc in the last ch (the one you made the first sc in) (10)
R2: (inc ×2, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc) ×2 (16)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Do not stuff.

### Ear — make 2 (left and right, identical)

Size before assembly: about 3.2 cm at the widest, 1.3 cm long; 5 rounds.

R1: with pink: 5 sc in MR (5)
R2: inc ×4, 1 sc (9)
R3: 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc (13)
R4: 1 sc, inc, 2 sc, inc, 2 sc, inc, 2 sc, inc, 2 sc (17)
R5: 17 sc (17)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Do not stuff. Shape it into a spoon: push the front wall into the back wall (not down to a thin plate) — a concave spoon about 3.3 mm deep in front, a rounded back, and rounded folds of two layers at the sides (about 5.7 mm thick); about 32 mm wide. Keep the opening a curve, hollow side forward, not a straight line.<!-- (spoon 50 %) -->

### Trotter — make 4

Of these: no. 1 — 2 (left and right); back — 2 (left and right).
Size before assembly: about 1.3 cm at the widest, 1.3 cm long; 4 rounds.

R1: with dark pink: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: (inc ×2, 1 sc) ×2 (10)
R3–4: 10 sc (10) — 2 rounds

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Stuff lightly, then press the piece slightly flat (oval cross-section; flattened 30 %).

### Cheek — make 2 (left and right, identical)

A flat circle about 1.5 cm across; 2 rounds.

R1: with dark pink: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: (inc ×2, 1 sc) ×2 (10)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Do not stuff.

## Assembly

Before sewing, mark the centre front of the round you need (the middle of the face / belly). “k st from the centre line” means count k stitches along that round. Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.
