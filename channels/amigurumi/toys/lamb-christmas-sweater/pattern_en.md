# Lamb in a Christmas Sweater — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 12.5 cm tall, 11.7 cm wide, 8.8 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - wool white `#f7f3ea` — about 85 m (2298 st)
  - sweater red `#c8303a` — about 25 m (726 st)
  - face tan `#e3c4a1` — about 15 m (410 st)
  - rib cream `#f3e9d6` — about 10 m (232 st)
  - snow white `#fbf8f2` — about 5 m (48 st)
  - hoof brown `#6b4a3a` — about 5 m (36 st)
  - blush `#f2998f` — about 5 m (36 st)
  - holly green `#3f8a4e` — about 5 m (22 st)
- Safety eyes 10 mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#151313`) — 2
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): nose pink `#f08e9e`, snow white `#fbf8f2`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |
| fpdc | front post dc: yarn over, hook around the post of the st from the front |
| bpdc | back post dc: yarn over, hook around the post of the st from the back |
| hdc | half double crochet |
| dc | double crochet |
| tr | treble crochet |
| picot | ch 3, sl st in the 3rd ch from hook |
| hdc2tog | 2 hdc worked together |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

## Pieces

### Body in sweater

Size before assembly: about 7.6 cm at the widest, 5.5 cm long; 19 rounds.

R1: with wool white: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
R3: (1 sc, inc) ×6 (18)
R4: 1 sc, (inc, 2 sc) ×5, inc, 1 sc (24)
R5: (3 sc, inc) ×6 (30)
R6: 2 sc, (inc, 4 sc) ×5, inc, 2 sc (36)
R7: (5 sc, inc) ×6 (42)
Change to sweater red (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R7).
R8: 3 sc, (inc, 6 sc) ×5, inc, 3 sc (48)
R9: (7 sc, inc) ×6 (54)
R10–16: 54 sc (54) — 7 rounds
Change to snow white (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R16).
R17: (7 sc, dec) ×6 (48)
Change to sweater red (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R17).
R18–19: 48 sc (48) — 2 rounds

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Stuff firmly just before sewing it on; a little less near the edge, then add a pinch when the seam is nearly done.

### Face

A flat oval about 6 × 4.4 cm; 6 rounds.
Worked around a chain, so it is an oval: sew it on with its long side (along the chain) running side to side. The round-start marker is in the middle of a long side (the piece's centre line), while R1 starts at a turn: after R1 shift the round start — sc 2 more (not counted in any round) and place the marker there. The increases fall on the turns.

With face tan: ch 6.
R1: starting in the 2nd ch from hook: sc 4, 3 sc in the last ch; continue along the other side of the chain: sc 3, 2 sc in the last ch (the one you made the first sc in) (12)
R2: inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 2 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc (18)
R3: 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 5 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 2 sc (24)
R4: 3 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 8 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 3 sc (30)
R5: 5 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 11 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 4 sc (36)
R6: 36 sc (36)

## Assembly

Before sewing, mark the centre front of the round you need (the middle of the face / belly). “k st from the centre line” means count k stitches along that round. Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.

1. **Body in sweater** is the base; its magic ring is at the bottom.
2. **Sweater hem → body in sweater**. Put the sweater hem around the body in sweater: the chain ring (56 st) lies along round 10 of the body in sweater, the rounds spreading down; sew the ring to the body in sweater all round and let the rest lie free on it. <!-- Exact placement: rounds grow straight down. -->
3. **Head onto the body in sweater.** Stuff the body in sweater firmly. Hold the head's closing point (last round) at the centre of the body in sweater's opening (R19) and sew the edge of the opening all round to the head: the seam runs along rounds 18–19 of the head (all the way round). Top up the neck with stuffing before closing the seam so the head does not wobble. Press it into the base by about 10 mm. <!-- Exact placement: axis 87.7° up, turned 90° to the toy's left; round-start line faces level, straight ahead; pressed in 10 mm; pressed toward 87.7° down, turned 90° to the toy's right. -->
