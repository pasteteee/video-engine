# Santa Stuck in a Chimney — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 13.9 cm tall, 9.4 cm wide, 7.9 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - brick `#b04a3c` — about 20 m (706 st)
  - white `#fbf8f1` — about 15 m (438 st)
  - red `#d8343a` — about 15 m (360 st)
  - black `#26201f` — about 10 m (300 st)
  - grey `#d3cbc0` — about 10 m (266 st)
- Safety eyes 13 mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#26201f`) — 2
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): black `#26201f`
- Dry pastel or blush for the cheeks (shade: pink `#ef9a8e`)
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

### Santa leg — make 2

Size before assembly: an oval about 3.3 × 2.1 cm at the widest, 7 cm long; 20 rounds.
The piece is oval: the rounds go around the chain, and on the finished toy the chain (the long side of the oval) runs front to back; the starting chain is at the top of the piece (on the finished toy). The round-start marker is at a turn of the oval, on the piece's centre line; R1 starts there, so no shift is needed. The increases fall on the turns.

With black: ch 5.
R1: starting in the 2nd ch from hook: sc 3, 3 sc in the last ch; continue along the other side of the chain: sc 2, 2 sc in the last ch (the one you made the first sc in) (10)
R2: (inc ×2, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc) ×2 (16)
R3: 1 sc, inc, 4 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 2 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc (22)
R4–6: 22 sc (22) — 3 rounds

Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.
