# Suspicious Side-Eye Dog — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 9.9 cm tall, 11.2 cm wide, 9.4 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - beige `#e2c08f` — about 45 m (1524 st)
  - brown `#8a5a3a` — about 15 m (353 st)
  - white `#ffffff` — about 10 m (230 st)
  - cream `#fbf1dc` — about 5 m (156 st)
- Safety eyes 7 mm (black `#1b1616`) — 1
- Safety eyes 7 mm (black `#1b1616`) — 1
- Safety nose 11×8 mm (oval; black `#1b1616`) — 1
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): dark brown `#4a3024`, grey `#5b5155`
- Felt: white (2 × 15×12.5 mm oval)
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

### Muzzle

Size before assembly: an oval about 2.7 × 1.9 cm at the widest, 1.5 cm long; 5 rounds.
The piece is oval: the rounds go around the chain, and on the finished toy the chain (the long side of the oval) runs side to side; the starting chain is at the front of the piece (on the finished toy). The round-start marker is in the middle of a long side (the piece's centre line), while R1 starts at a turn: after R1 shift the round start — sc 1 more (not counted in any round) and place the marker there. The increases fall on the turns.

With cream: ch 4.
R1: starting in the 2nd ch from hook: sc 2, 3 sc in the last ch; continue along the other side of the chain: sc 1, 2 sc in the last ch (the one you made the first sc in) (8)
R2: (inc ×3, 1 sc) ×2 (14)
R3: 2 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 5 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc (18)
R4–5: 18 sc (18) — 2 rounds

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Insert the safety nose/eyes now, while the piece is open (positions: see “Face”).
Stuff lightly just before sewing it on; a little less near the edge, then add a pinch when the seam is nearly done.

### Floppy ear — make 2 (left and right, identical)

Size before assembly: about 3 cm at the widest, 2.8 cm long; 9 rounds.

R1: with brown: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
R3: (1 sc, inc) ×6 (18)
R4–7: 18 sc (18) — 4 rounds
R8: 2 sc, (dec, 4 sc) ×2, dec, 2 sc (15)
R9: 15 sc (15)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Stuff it lightly — a thin layer, just enough to hold the walls apart: the ear about 10.8 mm thick, with thick rounded edges. Then shape it into a spoon: push the front wall toward the back wall — a concave spoon about 2.9 mm deep in front, a rounded back; about 30 mm wide. Keep the opening a curve, hollow side forward, not a straight line.<!-- (spoon 45 %) -->
Bend ≈ 90° around round 9<!-- the bend spreads over 3 rounds --><!-- drape relax_mm 6 twist 1 -->. The piece is soft and unstuffed: do not tack a fold. Sewn on as the assembly says, it bends over the head by itself and hangs down; the inner side of the bend faces down on the finished toy (on the piece: in round 9, 4 st from the centre front line, downward <!-- st 3.83 down -->). If the ear sticks out, smooth it down along the head by hand.

### Tail

Size before assembly: about 1.1 cm at the widest, 3.4 cm long; 10 rounds.

R1: with brown: 5 sc in MR (5)
R2: inc ×2, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc (8)
R3–10: 8 sc (8) — 8 rounds
