# Sleepy Sloth Ball — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 5.6 cm tall, 7.8 cm wide, 7 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - tan `#c79a6a` — about 25 m (774 st)
  - cream `#f3e4c8` — about 5 m (140 st)
  - brown `#7a5436` — about 5 m (60 st)
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): dark brown `#2a1a12`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- The face is fully embroidered — no hard parts, safe for small children.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

## Pieces

### Sloth ball

Size before assembly: about 6.6 cm at the widest, 5.4 cm long; 22 rounds.

R1: with tan: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
R3: (1 sc, inc) ×6 (18)
R4: 1 sc, (inc, 2 sc) ×5, inc, 1 sc (24)
R5: (3 sc, inc) ×6 (30)
R6: 2 sc, (inc, 4 sc) ×5, inc, 2 sc (36)
R7: (5 sc, inc) ×6 (42)
R8: 3 sc, (inc, 6 sc) ×5, inc, 3 sc (48)
R9–14: 48 sc (48) — 6 rounds
R15: (6 sc, dec) ×6 (42)
R16: 42 sc (42)
R17: (5 sc, dec) ×6 (36)
R18: 2 sc, (dec, 4 sc) ×5, dec, 2 sc (30)
R19: (3 sc, dec) ×6 (24)

→ Start stuffing (firmly) and keep adding as you decrease.

R20: 1 sc, (dec, 2 sc) ×5, dec, 1 sc (18)
R21: (1 sc, dec) ×6 (12)
R22: dec ×6 (6)

Top up the stuffing if needed. Fasten off leaving a 15 cm tail; with the needle weave it through the front loops of all 6 stitches of the last round, pull tight and hide the end inside.

### Cream face mask

A flat oval about 6.1 × 3.7 cm; 5 rounds.
Worked around a chain, so it is an oval: sew it on with its long side (along the chain) running side to side. The round-start marker is in the middle of a long side (the piece's centre line), while R1 starts at a turn: after R1 shift the round start — sc 3 more (not counted in any round) and place the marker there. The increases fall on the turns.

With cream: ch 8.
R1: starting in the 2nd ch from hook: sc 6, 3 sc in the last ch; continue along the other side of the chain: sc 5, 2 sc in the last ch (the one you made the first sc in) (16)
R2: 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 4 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc (22)
R3: 3 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 7 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 2 sc (28)
R4: 4 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 10 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 4 sc (34)
R5: 5 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 13 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 6 sc (40)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Do not stuff.

### Dark eye patch — make 2 (left and right, identical)

A flat circle about 2.3 cm across; 3 rounds.

R1: with brown: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
R3: 12 sc (12)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Do not stuff.

### Arm — make 2 (left and right, identical)

Size before assembly: about 1.1 cm at the widest, 1.3 cm long; 4 rounds.

R1: with tan: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: 1 sc, inc, 2 sc, inc, 1 sc (8)
R3–4: 8 sc (8) — 2 rounds

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Stuff lightly just before sewing it on; a little less near the edge, then add a pinch when the seam is nearly done.

## Assembly

Before sewing, mark the centre front of the round you need (the middle of the face / belly). “k st from the centre line” means count k stitches along that round. Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.

1. **Sloth ball** is the base; its magic ring is at the bottom.
2. **Cream face mask → sloth ball**. Centre: between rounds 12 and 13, on the centre front line. Sew it flat all round its edge; it covers rounds 9–16 of the sloth ball, about 12 st wide. <!-- Exact placement: its top (round-start line) faces 87.6° up, straight back; raised 1 mm. -->
3. **Dark eye patch → sloth ball** (make 2). Sew it flat all round its edge; it covers rounds 10–15 of the sloth ball, about 5 st wide. <!-- Exact placement: its top (round-start line) faces 69.9° up, turned 72.6° to the toy's right; raised 2.4 mm. -->
4. **Arm → sloth ball** (make 2). Centre: between rounds 8 and 9, 11.5 st from the centre front line, toward the toy's left <!-- st 11.73 left --> (the one on the toy's left is described; sew the other as its mirror image).
