# Shark in a Hoodie — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 11.1 cm tall, 12.2 cm wide, 8.9 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - pink `#f0809f` — about 55 m (1784 st)
  - dark pink `#cf5a7c` — about 15 m (456 st)
  - grey `#7d93a6` — about 15 m (411 st)
  - white `#f3f1ec` — about 5 m (61 st)
  - light pink `#f9b4c4` — about 5 m (10 st)
- Safety eyes 10–12<!-- 11 mm --> mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#151313`) — 2
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): rose `#9c3f52`, white `#f3f1ec`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

## Assembly

Before sewing, mark the centre front of the round you need (the middle of the face / belly). “k st from the centre line” means count k stitches along that round. Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.

1. **Hoodie body** is the base; its magic ring is at the bottom.
2. **Head (hood) onto the hoodie body.** Stuff the hoodie body firmly. Hold the head (hood)'s closing point (last round) at the centre of the hoodie body's opening (R15) and sew the edge of the opening all round to the head (hood): the seam runs along round 20 of the head (hood) (all the way round). Top up the neck with stuffing before closing the seam so the head does not wobble. Press it into the base by about 12 mm. <!-- Exact placement: axis straight up; round-start line faces level, straight ahead; pressed in 12 mm; pressed toward straight down. -->
3. **Hood trim → head (hood)**. Centre: in round 13, on the centre front line. Sew it flat all round its edge; it covers rounds 7–19 of the head (hood), about 16 st wide. <!-- Exact placement: its top (round-start line) faces 81° up, straight back; padded with stuffing 1.5 mm thick; raised 0.8 mm. -->
4. **Shark face → head (hood)**. Centre: in round 13, on the centre front line. Sew it flat all round its edge; it covers rounds 8–18 of the head (hood), about 16 st wide.
