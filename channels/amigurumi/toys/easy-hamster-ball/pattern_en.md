# Round Hamster — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 8.3 cm tall, 8.5 cm wide, 8.5 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - ginger `#e9a35c` — about 25 m (804 st)
  - cream `#fff0d4` — about 15 m (434 st)
  - pink `#ff9eb0` — about 5 m (32 st)
- Safety eyes 9 mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#151313`) — 2
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): brown `#5a3420`, pink `#ff9eb0`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

### Ear — make 2 (left and right, identical)

Size before assembly: about 2.3 cm at the widest, 1.5 cm long; 5 rounds.

R1: with ginger: 5 sc in MR (5)
R2: inc ×5 (10)
R3–4: 10 sc (10) — 2 rounds
R5: (3 sc, dec) ×2 (8)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Do not stuff. Shape it into a dish: rounds 1–2 are the front of the piece, the magic ring its centre. Push the centre in so the front is concave (about 2.7 mm at the centre) and turn rounds 3–5 to the back: they make a thick rounded rim (about 8 mm thick) and lie behind as a second layer; the piece is about 23 mm across.<!-- (dish 70 %) -->

### Foot — make 2 (left and right, identical)

Size before assembly: about 1.6 cm at the widest, 1.2 cm long; 4 rounds.

R1: with cream: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
R3–4: 12 sc (12) — 2 rounds

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Stuff lightly only and press it flat to about 6 mm thick.<!-- flattened 60 % -->

### Cheek — make 2 (left and right, identical)

A flat circle about 1.5 cm across; 2 rounds.

R1: with pink: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: (inc ×2, 1 sc) ×2 (10)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Do not stuff.

## Assembly

Before sewing, mark the centre front of the round you need (the middle of the face / belly). “k st from the centre line” means count k stitches along that round. Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.

1. **Hamster ball** is the base; its magic ring is at the bottom.
2. **Ear → hamster ball** (make 2). Centre: in round 24, 4 st from the centre front line, toward the toy's left <!-- st 4.13 left --> (the one on the toy's left is described; sew the other as its mirror image). Stand the dish on its rim, hollow side forward, and sew it on by the lower arc of the rim — about 3 st of round 4, catching the back layer too; the seam covers rounds 23–25 of the hamster ball, about 1 st wide. Press it into the base by about 4 mm. <!-- Exact placement: front face looks level, straight ahead; it stands out 73.3° up, turned 90° to the toy's left; pressed in 4 mm. -->
3. **Foot → hamster ball** (make 2). Centre: in round 3, 1.5 st from the centre front line, toward the toy's left <!-- st 1.4 left --> (the one on the toy's left is described; sew the other as its mirror image). Flatten the opening (6 st against 6 st) and sew it on in a straight line side to side (across the toy), along the round: rounds 3–5 of the hamster ball, about 6 st wide. It points forward. <!-- Exact placement: axis 5.5° down, turned 14° to the toy's left; round-start line faces 84.5° up, turned 14° to the toy's left. -->
4. **Cheek → hamster ball** (make 2). Centre: in round 17, 8.5 st from the centre front line, toward the toy's left <!-- st 8.67 left rnd 17.2 --> (the one on the toy's left is described; sew the other as its mirror image). Sew it flat all round its edge; it covers rounds 15–19 of the hamster ball, about 4 st wide.
