# Mini Stocking Ornament — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 14.6 cm tall, 7.2 cm wide, 6.1 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - red `#d63a34` — about 30 m (974 st)
  - white `#fbf9f4` — about 15 m (362 st)
  - green `#2f7d4a` — about 5 m (106 st)
  - pink `#ffa3b5` — about 5 m (32 st)
- Safety eyes 9 mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#1c1a1a`) — 1
- Safety eyes 9 mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#1c1a1a`) — 1
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): brown `#4a3a34`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

## Pieces

### Stocking

Size before assembly: about 5.9 cm at the widest, 11 cm long; 35 rounds.
Multi-colour rounds are listed stitch by stitch from the round-start marker; keep the round start on the piece's centre top line (as it sits on the finished toy). The order is for right-handed crocheting (right to left on the outside); left-handed: reverse it.

R1: with white: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc ×6 (12)
Change to red (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R2).
R3: (1 sc, inc) ×6 (18)
R4: 1 sc, (inc, 2 sc) ×5, inc, 1 sc (24)
R5: (3 sc, inc) ×6 (30)
R6: 2 sc, (inc, 4 sc) ×5, inc, 2 sc (36)
R7: (5 sc, inc) ×6 (42)
R8–10: 42 sc (42) — 3 rounds
R11: 18 sc in red, 4 sc in white, 20 sc in red (42)
R12: 16 sc in red, 8 sc in white, 18 sc in red (42)
R13: 15 sc in red, 9 sc in white, 18 sc in red (42)
R14: 15 sc in red, 10 sc in white, 17 sc in red (42)
R15: 15 sc in red, 9 sc in white, 18 sc in red (42)
R16: 16 sc in red, 7 sc in white, 19 sc in red (42)
R17: 18 sc in red, 3 sc in white, 21 sc in red (42)
R18–26: 42 sc (42) — 9 rounds
Change to green (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R26).
R27: 42 sc (42)
Change to white (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R27).
R28–31: 42 sc (42) — 4 rounds
R32: (1 sc, dec, 1 sc, dec, 1 sc) ×6 (30)
R33: (dec ×2, 1 sc) ×6 (18)

→ Insert the safety eyes now (positions: see “Face”) and fix the backs. Start stuffing (firmly) and keep adding as you decrease.

R34: (1 sc, dec) ×6 (12)
R35: dec ×6 (6)

Top up the stuffing if needed. Fasten off leaving a 15 cm tail; with the needle weave it through the front loops of all 6 stitches of the last round, pull tight and hide the end inside.
Bend ≈ 90° around round 14<!-- the bend spreads over 5 rounds -->. After stuffing, bend the piece so the inner side of the bend faces up on the finished toy (on the piece: in round 14, on the centre top line). It is a gradual bend: with the needle and matching yarn run a line of stitches along the inner side from round 8 to round 21, one stitch in every round along one column, then pull the yarn until the piece bends to the angle (the inner side gathers by about 13 rounds) and fasten off inside.

### Candy cane

Size before assembly: about 1.3 cm at the widest, 4.9 cm long; 14 rounds.

R1: with red: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: (inc ×2, 1 sc) ×2 (10)
Change to white (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R2).
R3–4: 10 sc (10) — 2 rounds
Change to red (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R4).
R5–6: 10 sc (10) — 2 rounds
Change to white (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R6).
R7–8: 10 sc (10) — 2 rounds
Change to red (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R8).
R9–10: 10 sc (10) — 2 rounds
Change to white (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R10).
R11–12: 10 sc (10) — 2 rounds
Change to red (pull it through on the last yarn-over of R12).
R13–14: 10 sc (10) — 2 rounds

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Stuff lightly just before sewing it on; a little less near the edge, then add a pinch when the seam is nearly done.
Bend ≈ 160° around round 5<!-- the bend spreads over 5 rounds -->. After stuffing, bend the piece so the inner side of the bend faces toward the toy's left on the finished toy (on the piece: in round 5, 2.5 st from the centre front line, toward the toy's left). It is a gradual bend: with the needle and matching yarn run a line of stitches along the inner side from round 2 to round 7, one stitch in every round along one column, then pull the yarn until the piece bends to the angle (the inner side gathers by about 5 rounds) and fasten off inside.

### Cheek — make 2

A flat circle about 1.5 cm across; 2 rounds.

R1: with pink: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: (inc ×2, 1 sc) ×2 (10)

Fasten off leaving a long tail (30–40 cm) for sewing.
Do not stuff.

### Hanging loop

Size before assembly: about 2 cm at the widest, 0.7 cm long; 2 rounds.
Each piece is two circles (front and back): make 2 circles — 1 as below and 1 plain in green (same rounds, no colour changes).

R1: with green: ch 16 and join into a ring with a sl st in the first ch (do not twist); 16 sc, one in each ch of the ring (16)
R2: 16 sc (16)

Fasten off. Hold a front and a plain circle wrong sides together and sc around through both layers: 16 sc. Do not stuff. Leave a long tail (~30 cm) for sewing.

Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.
