# LoreSquare channel: style "Ink"

Chosen by the owner on 2026-11-15 for the theory Shorts. Reference frames: the six
Shorts in `/mnt/project-files/shorts/series/<slug>/` (8 key frames each, EN and RU)
and their generator in `/mnt/project-files/shorts/series/source/`.

## Look
- **Slam frames.** One colour field (red `#e63946` with speed lines, black `#0b0b0b`,
  blue `#1f6feb` or a duotone manga panel) and 2-3 lines of all-caps Oswald 700.
  Hook, context, twist and CTA are slams.
- **Case files.** Aged paper poster pinned on a dark wood ground: WANTED for the
  theory and its author, EXHIBIT N for a clue, COUNTERPOINT for the defence.
  Headers in Playfair Display SC 900, lines in PT Mono.
- **Stamps.** Rotated rubber stamps carry what must be read: `CH. 1192`,
  `THEORY BY @handle`, `70% BUY IT`, `BUT...`. Stamp red `#b3121f`, green `#1b6b2a`,
  navy `#1b3a8a`.
- **Claim stamps (long videos).** Every claim card says what it is:
  CANON green `#2f9e44` (with chapter), INFERENCE yellow `#f0b400`,
  THEORY pink `#e64980`, COMMUNITY blue `#1f6feb`.
- **Manga panels.** Duotone (blue or red) or inside a case-file frame, never a bare
  page. Crop to the detail, push in slowly, our circle/arrow/note lands on the
  sync word. 3-5 s, never two panels in a row, at most a third of the runtime.
- **Captions.** White Rubik 800 at the bottom, the key word in yellow `#ffd43b`.
  One phrase at a time; never over a card's text.
- **Credit.** `One Piece © Eiichiro Oda / Shueisha` in small PT Mono bottom-left of
  every frame that shows a panel (and on every Short frame).
- **Logo.** LoreSquare mark top-right, small.
