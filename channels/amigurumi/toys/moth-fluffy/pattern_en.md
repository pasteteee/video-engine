# Fluffy Moth — crochet pattern

**Finished size:** about 14.6 cm tall, 18.1 cm wide, 9.9 cm deep (at the stated gauge).

## Materials

- DK weight yarn (about 250 m/100 g), cotton or acrylic; 2.5 mm hook (smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a tight fabric).
- Colours (amounts are an estimate: stitches × 2.4 cm per sc + sewing tails + 15 %):
  - cream `#f7ecd6` — about 50 m (1630 st)
  - beige `#ead7b4` — about 30 m (856 st)
  - tan `#cfae7e` — about 15 m (476 st)
  - white `#fffaf0` — about 15 m (468 st)
  - brown `#8a6a4c` — about 10 m (178 st)
  - pink `#f2a39a` — about 5 m (30 st)
  - dark brown `#4a3830` — about 5 m (12 st)
- Safety eyes 12 mm with a white glint (cartoon eyes) (black `#1e1a1a`) — 2
- Embroidery thread (floss or thin yarn): dark brown `#4a3830`
- Fibrefill stuffing, a stitch marker, a yarn needle, pins, scissors.
- Safety eyes and noses are not safe for children under 3 — embroider them instead.

## Gauge

1 sc ≈ 4 mm wide, 1 round ≈ 3.6 mm tall (10 cm ≈ 25 sc × 28 rounds); yarn about 1.9 mm thick. The gauge sets the size of the toy; crochet tightly so the stuffing does not show.

## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| MR | magic ring |
| sc | single crochet |
| inc | 2 sc in the same stitch |
| dec | single crochet two stitches together (invisible decrease recommended) |
| BLO | work the round in back loops only |
| FLO | work the round in front loops only |
| ch | chain |
| sl st | slip stitch |
| st | stitch |
| R5 | round 5; R7–14 = rounds 7 to 14 are the same |
| (…) ×6 | repeat 6 times |
| (24) | stitches in the round when it is done |
| popcorn | 5 dc in one stitch; take the hook out of the loop, insert it in the first of the 5 dc, catch the dropped loop and pull it through - the dc gather into a bobble on the right side |
| hdc | half double crochet |
| dc | double crochet |
| tr | treble crochet |
| picot | ch 3, sl st in the 3rd ch from hook |
| hdc2tog | 2 hdc worked together |

Work in a continuous spiral, no joins and no turning chains; mark the first stitch of every round. Left/right always means the toy's own left/right (the toy's left is on your right when it faces you).

Colour changes: pull the new colour through on the last yarn-over of the previous stitch. Carry the unused colour loosely on the inside. “[white] …” means: everything up to the next bracket in that colour.

### Face patch

A flat oval about 5.2 × 3.6 cm; 5 rounds.
Worked around a chain, so it is an oval: sew it on with its long side (along the chain) running side to side. The round-start marker is in the middle of a long side (the piece's centre line), while R1 starts at a turn: after R1 shift the round start — sc 2 more (not counted in any round) and place the marker there. The increases fall on the turns.

With beige: ch 6.
R1: starting in the 2nd ch from hook: sc 4, 3 sc in the last ch; continue along the other side of the chain: sc 3, 2 sc in the last ch (the one you made the first sc in) (12)
R2: 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 4 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc (16)
R3: 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 4 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc (22)
R4: 3 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 7 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 2 sc (28)
R5: 4 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 10 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 4 sc (34)

Insert the safety nose/eyes now, while the piece is open (positions: see “Face”).
Do not stuff.

## Assembly

Pin everything first and compare with the picture, then sew with the tail left on each piece, one stitch of the piece and one of the base at a time.

1. **Body** is the base; its magic ring is at the bottom.
2. **Foot → body** (make 2). Centre: in round 5, 2.5 st from the centre front line, toward the toy's left (the one on the toy's left is described; sew the other as its mirror image). Sew around the opening (10 st); the seam covers rounds 4–7 of the body, about 4 st wide.
